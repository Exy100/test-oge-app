```python

t = int(input())
A = int(input())
if s > A or t > 7:
    print("YES")
else:
    print("NO")
```

| s   | t   |
| --- | --- |
| 6   | -1  |
| -1  | 12  |
| -7  | 5   |
| 9   | 2   |
| -7  | 8   |
